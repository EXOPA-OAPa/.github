## About us

EXOPA studies exoplanetary systems with a multidisciplinary approach, investigating how the host star's environment shapes planetary architecture. Our research spans the detection and characterization of exoplanets, stellar activity, exoplanetary atmospheres, star–planet interaction, laboratory simulations of planetary atmospheres, and technologies for space instrumentation. The group is involved in missions and programmes such as Ariel, PLATO, ESPRESSO and HARPS-N/GAPS.

This organization hosts the open-source codes, numerical models and analysis tools developed by the group.
